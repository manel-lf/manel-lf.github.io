One step in a case study's process scroller. Put a series inside `<div className="scroller">`.

```jsx
<div className="scroller">
  <ProcessCard index={1} title="Funnel teardown" meta="Product analytics"
    body="Instrumented the first session step by step and found the drop was not at paywall or signup." />
</div>
```

Bodies say what was done and what it revealed — never "we explored" or "we ideated". Six cards is the house length.
