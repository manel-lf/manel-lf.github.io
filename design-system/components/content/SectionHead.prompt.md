Opens every section: a muted label followed by an ink statement, same size, same weight.

```jsx
<SectionHead headingId="work-h" label="Recent work." statement="From pixels to products." />
<SectionHead label="Shoutouts." statement="What stakeholders and teammates say about me."
  aside="People I have worked with directly, across product, design and engineering." />
```

The two-part structure is the brand's headline signature — a category, then a claim, both punctuated with a full stop. Do not use a single-line heading instead, and do not size the label smaller than the statement.
