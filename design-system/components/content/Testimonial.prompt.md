A full testimonial: hairline quote mark, the quote, then avatar and attribution.

```jsx
<Testimonial
  quote="Manel combines strong design skills with genuine product thinking…"
  name="Emmi Kuusikko" role="Head of Product"
  avatar="assets/img/testimonial-emmi-kuusikko.jpg"
  href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/emmik/" />
```

Quotes run in full, in the speaker's own words — the site does not trim them into pull-quotes. Pair with a `.quoteFoot` row of `.dot` indicators when you carousel several.
